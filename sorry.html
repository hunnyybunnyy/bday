04 / one last thing

Something I want to say.

Not to change your birthday mood. Just something honest, from my heart.

dear honey,

I'm sorry. ♡

I'm sorry for the times I got too attached and forgot to give you the space you need. For the times I said things without thinking, or let my feelings become bigger than the moment.

I know caring about someone also means respecting their boundaries, listening properly, and understanding that quiet time is important too. I am learning to do that better.

I never wanted to make things feel heavy for you. I take responsibility for the words and moments I could have handled with more care.

But, unfortunately, it is all because of how much I care about us. I want us to be together, forever. So, when I sense something different, I say it out loud, which causes problems sometimes.

But, I want us to honest with each other. I want us to be each other's safe space! Not a place where we feel judged. So, I am going to work on myself and learn with you! It is life, we all make mistakes- but it is good to learn from them!

Today is still about celebrating you. I hope your birthday is full of happiness, peace, and all the things you love.

I love you, always ♡
